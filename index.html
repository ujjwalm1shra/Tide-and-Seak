FAQ

Common questions.

Tide&Seak is a freight intelligence and chartering decision-support platform for bulk cargo procurement, focused on voyages into India's East Coast.
Predict is built to forecast freight rate movement across vessel classes, routes and time horizons, helping teams anticipate market direction before fixing a vessel.
The platform is designed around common bulk vessel classes such as Supramax, Panamax and Capesize, evaluated against cargo and port requirements.
The current focus spans origins including Australia, the United States, Mozambique, Indonesia and Russia, into East Coast Indian ports such as Paradip, Visakhapatnam, Gangavaram, Gopalpur, Dhamra, Haldia and Sagar-Sandheads.
Port intelligence factors such as draft, LOA, beam and handling capacity are considered alongside market and vessel data to assess practical vessel-to-port fit.
Predict forecasts where freight rates are heading. Simulate lets you test specific chartering strategies — such as charter-now versus charter-later — against those conditions.
Tide&Seak is a decision-support platform. It informs chartering and procurement decisions rather than executing fixtures directly.
